MONKE GORE

One of you becomes the monster. Bigger, deeper-voiced, and hungry. Everyone else survives until the clock runs out.

ONLINE ROOM
Create a room and share its code, or enter a friend's code.

Desktop: WASD move, Space jump, mouse look, V third person. Left/right click grabs. Hold F to pull. Use the lobby computer or keyboard to change your name/color, start a round, or choose Monster Practice.
VR: push off surfaces with your fists, grip/trigger to climb and grab. Thumbstick = snap turn. Poke the lobby keyboard or point + trigger at the computer.
Monster: pick up survivors by the body, carry them, and pull limbs away to tear them off. Dead characters collapse into physics ragdolls. Online: create a code, send it to others, and keep the host's browser open.

Online play uses PeerJS/WebRTC peer-to-peer networking. Network access and browser NAT/firewall settings can affect whether a connection succeeds.
